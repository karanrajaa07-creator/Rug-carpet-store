The Bespoke Method

Four Steps to a Bespoke Masterpiece

From initial conceptual CAD sketches to the final celebratory unwrapping in your home.

1
Spatial & Lighting Assessment

We analyze your architectural drawings, ambient lighting conditions, and furniture placement to propose optimal dimensions and pile density.

2
Yarn Selection & Strike-Off Sample

Select your raw fibers from over 400 vegetable dye swatches. We weave a physical 1' × 1' strike-off sample for on-site approval.

3
Artisan Hand-Loom Weaving

Master weavers in our partner ateliers meticulously tie each individual knot by hand, sending you bi-weekly photographic progress reports.

4
Insured White-Glove Installation

Our installation specialists place the rug, trim custom natural rubber underlay pads, and vacuum to perfection.
