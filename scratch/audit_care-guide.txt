          <h4 class="font-serif fw-bold text-terracotta mb-3">Highland Virgin Wool Care</h4>
          <p class="text-muted leading-relaxed mb-3">

          </ul>

        <div class="tab-pane fade p-4 luxury-card" id="silkCare" role="tabpanel">

          <p class="text-muted leading-relaxed mb-3">
            Silk is an exquisite, protein-based filament that requires the most delicate handling in the rug universe.
          </p>
          <ul class="list-unstyled d-flex flex-column gap-2 small text-muted">
            <li><i class="bi bi-check2 text-success me-2"></i> Ideal for low-foot-traffic chambers: primary bedroom suites, private libraries, and formal salons.</li>
            <li><i class="bi bi-check2 text-success me-2"></i> For dust removal, vacuum with a soft bristle brush attachment or manually sweep with a natural corn broom.</li>
            <li><i class="bi bi-x-circle text-danger me-2"></i> <strong>Never rub a wet spill on silk.</strong> Friction permanently fractures the microscopic triangular silk prism fibers, causing dull spots.</li>
          </ul>

          <p class="text-muted leading-relaxed mb-3">
            Jute and plant fibers are derived from plant stems and are hydrophilic (they absorb moisture readily).
          </p>
          <ul class="list-unstyled d-flex flex-column gap-2 small text-muted">

            <li><i class="bi bi-x-circle text-danger me-2"></i> Keep away from wet areas like bathrooms or unshielded outdoor decks. Water will cause water-marking browning.</li>
            <li><i class="bi bi-check2 text-success me-2"></i> For dry soil, sprinkle baking soda, let sit 20 minutes, then vacuum thoroughly.</li>

  <section class="py-5">
    <div class="container py-4">
      <div class="text-center max-w-700 mx-auto mb-5">
        <div class="section-tag justify-content-center">First-Aid Guide</div>
        <h2 class="section-title">Emergency Stain Removal Protocols</h2>
        <p class="section-desc mx-auto">Act swiftly: 90% of stains can be completely erased if blotted within the first 3 minutes.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="table-responsive max-w-900 mx-auto">
        <table class="table table-bordered luxury-card">
          <thead class="bg-subtle font-serif">
            <tr>
              <th>Stain Type</th>
              <th>Immediate First Action</th>
              <th>Artisan Cleaning Solution</th>
            </tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody class="small">
            <tr>
              <td class="fw-bold">Red Wine / Fruit Juice</td>
              <td>Blot repeatedly with dry white towel. Do not rub.</td>
              <td>Mix 1 part white vinegar + 2 parts lukewarm water with a drop of mild dish soap. Dab gently from outside edge inward.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
              <td class="fw-bold">Coffee / Tea</td>
              <td>Blot excess liquid immediately.</td>
              <td>Dab with mild wool detergent mixed with cold water. Blot dry, then place a weighted towel over the spot for 2 hours.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
              <td class="fw-bold">Oils / Grease / Butter</td>
              <td>Scrape solids with a dull butter knife.</td>
              <td>Dust generously with cornstarch or talcum powder. Allow 4 hours to absorb grease, then vacuum suction-only.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
              <td class="fw-bold">Pet Accidents</td>
              <td>Blot thoroughly with layered paper towels.</td>
              <td>Apply a solution of 1/4 cup white vinegar + 2 cups cold water to neutralize odor and acid. Rinse with cold water dab.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
              <td class="fw-bold">Candle Wax</td>
              <td>Allow wax to harden fully (use ice cube).</td>
              <td>Gently shatter and pick off chunks. Place brown paper bag over remaining residue and lightly press with warm (not hot) iron.</td>
            </tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>

  <!-- WHAT NOT TO DO -->
  <section class="py-5 bg-dark-luxury text-white">
    <div class="container py-4">
      <div class="text-center max-w-700 mx-auto mb-5">
        <span class="badge bg-danger px-3 py-2 text-uppercase fw-bold mb-3">Critical Precautions</span>
        <h2 class="display-5 font-serif fw-bold text-white">What Never to Do to a Handwoven Rug</h2>
      </div>

      <div class="row g-4">
        <div class="col-md-4">
          <div class="p-4 rounded-4 border border-secondary bg-black bg-opacity-25 h-100">
            <i class="bi bi-slash-circle text-danger fs-2 mb-3 d-block"></i>
            <h5 class="font-serif text-white mb-2">No Steam Cleaners</h5>
            <p class="small text-muted mb-0">High-temperature pressurized steam forces moisture deep into the inner wool foundation warps, causing dry rot and fiber mold.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-4">
          <div class="p-4 rounded-4 border border-secondary bg-black bg-opacity-25 h-100">
            <i class="bi bi-slash-circle text-danger fs-2 mb-3 d-block"></i>
            <h5 class="font-serif text-white mb-2">No Supermarket Chemical Sprays</h5>
            <p class="small text-muted mb-0">Bleach, ammonia, and foam stain removers chemically strip natural vegetable dyes and leave permanent bleached halos.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-4">
          <div class="p-4 rounded-4 border border-secondary bg-black bg-opacity-25 h-100">
            <i class="bi bi-slash-circle text-danger fs-2 mb-3 d-block"></i>
            <h5 class="font-serif text-white mb-2">Never Vacuum The Fringe</h5>
            <p class="small text-muted mb-0">The fringe cords are the structural warp foundation of the rug. Vacuum heads catch and unravel end knots instantly.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
