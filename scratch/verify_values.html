Pillars of Integrity

Our Guiding Values

Ethical Fair Compensation

Our weavers receive living wages, healthcare endowments, and safe daylight loom workshops.

Vegetable Mineral Dyes

Walnut shells, madder root, indigo, and pomegranate rinds create timeless abrash shades that never emit VOCs.

Century Durability

Double-weft hand-knotted architecture designed to outlive machine-made rugs by several generations.

100% Biodegradable

Crafted from earth-given wool, cotton, and silk fibers that return organically to the soil without synthetic microplastics.
