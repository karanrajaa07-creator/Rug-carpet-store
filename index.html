Spatial Proportions

How to Choose The Perfect Rug Size

Selecting the ideal dimensions grounds your room, establishes intimate zones, and enhances acoustic warmth.

Living Room Layouts

Ensure front legs of all sofas and armchairs rest comfortably on the rug to tie the conversation zone together.

Recommended Sizes:
8' × 10' (Medium) 9' × 12' (Spacious) 10' × 14' (Grand)
Dining Table Settings

Leave at least 24 inches of rug perimeter beyond all sides of the table so chairs remain on the rug when pulled out.

Recommended Sizes:
8' × 10' (6 Chairs) 9' × 12' (8 Chairs) 10' × 14' (10-12 Chairs)
Bedroom Sanctuaries

Place the rug perpendicular under the lower two-thirds of the bed, extending 18 to 24 inches on both sides for soft morning footing.

Recommended Sizes:
6' × 9' (Queen Bed) 8' × 10' (King Bed) 9' × 12' (Grand Suite)
