Bespoke Commission

Custom Rug Design & Handweaving

Create an original heirloom textile scaled exactly to your room proportions, fiber tactile preferences, and interior chromatic scheme.

The Art of Commissioning a Bespoke Rug

A bespoke rug is an investment in spatial harmony. Rather than compromising on mass-market standard sizes that leave irregular floor margins or conflicting dye lots, our atelier allows you to dictate every nuance — from pile height and knot density to the selection of botanical vegetable dyes.

Core Benefits of Our Bespoke Service

Flawless Architectural Proportion

Custom dimensioning guarantees uniform border reveals around sofas, hearths, and dining tables.

Precision Color Matching

Yarns are hand-dyed to match your paint swatches, wallpaper accents, and drapery fabrics.

Tailored Fiber Blends

Select high-traffic resilient wool, ultra-plush bamboo silk, or durable organic jute depending on room function.

Verified Weaver Provenance

Every commissioned rug includes a certificate stamped with weaver collective signature and knot registry.

The Commission Journey

1
Spatial Consultation & Moodboard

We review architectural plans, lighting orientation, and desired mood, suggesting suitable knot density and pile finishes.

2
Scale CAD Rendering & Strike-Off Sample

We generate a full-color digital CAD drawing. Upon approval, we weave a 1' × 1' physical corner strike-off sample delivered to your door.

3
Loom Warping & Hand-Knotting

Master artisans warp vertical timber looms and hand-tie every individual knot using high-twist organic wool and mulberry silk.

4
Washing, Shearing & Sun-Curing

The rug undergoes 3 submerged artisan washes with natural soaps, blade hand-shearing to precise millimeter pile heights, and open-air terrace curing.

5
White-Glove Installation

Insured global courier delivery, room placement, padding adjustments, and post-installation vacuum care.
