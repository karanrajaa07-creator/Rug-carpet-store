Centuries of Safavid Symmetry

The Isfahan medallion design is revered throughout textile history for its mathematical precision and natural curvilinear vine tendrils. Each medallion is plotted by hand on paper cartouches before weaving begins, representing over four months of focused knotting by two generational weavers working concurrently.

The pure Mulberry silk yarn used in the central flora catches natural ambient light dynamically. In low evening lighting, the rug glows with deep copper and terracotta warmth; under morning sun, it reveals shimmering champagne and ecru accents.

Daily Maintenance

Vacuum once weekly using suction-only mode without rotating beater brushes. Rotate the rug 180 degrees every six months to ensure even wear across sunny and high-traffic zones.

Spills & Emergencies

Blot immediately with a dry white cotton towel — never scrub silk fibers. For wine or coffee, use lukewarm water with a drop of mild wool shampoo.
