Textile Conservation

Rug Care & Longevity Handbook

A handmade rug woven from living animal and plant fibers can thrive for over a century when cared for properly. Learn foundational maintenance rules from our conservators.

Routine Stewardship

The Four Golden Rules of Daily Care

Suction-Only Vacuuming

Disengage rotating roller beater brushes. Harsh revolving bristles pull and frizz delicate hand-spun wool fibers.

Bi-Annual Rotation

Rotate your rug 180 degrees every six months to distribute foot traffic patterns and balance natural UV sunlight exposure.

Quality Rug Underlay

Always use a dual-surface natural rubber and felt rug pad. Pads absorb heel impact and prevent foundation warp.

Sunlight Filtration

Use sheer curtains or low-E glass treatments to shield natural vegetable dyes from harsh direct solar ultraviolet rays.

Fiber Intelligence

Care Instructions by Material

Highland Virgin Wool Care

Natural wool contains lanolin, a waxy barrier that natively repels liquids and stains for 10 to 15 minutes before absorption occurs.

  • Initial shedding is normal for high-pile rugs during the first 2-3 months — gently vacuum in the pile direction.
  • Blot liquid spills immediately with clean dry white paper towels.
  • Never use hot water or alkaline detergents; high heat shrinks wool fibers and strips natural lanolin.

Pure Mulberry Silk Care

Silk is an exquisite, protein-based filament that requires the most delicate handling in the rug universe.

  • Ideal for low-foot-traffic chambers: primary bedroom suites, private libraries, and formal salons.
  • For dust removal, vacuum with a soft bristle brush attachment or manually sweep with a natural corn broom.
  • Never rub a wet spill on silk. Friction permanently fractures the microscopic triangular silk prism fibers, causing dull spots.

Organic Jute & Sisal Care

Jute and plant fibers are derived from plant stems and are hydrophilic (they absorb moisture readily).

  • Vacuum weekly to remove abrasive sand grit that can sever plant fiber knots.
  • Keep away from wet areas like bathrooms or unshielded outdoor decks. Water will cause water-marking browning.
  • For dry soil, sprinkle baking soda, let sit 20 minutes, then vacuum thoroughly.

Pure Cotton Flatweave Care

Cotton flatweaves and kilims offer relaxed versatility and reversible longevity.

  • Reversible design: flip the rug annually to enjoy a fresh identical surface.
  • Small flatweaves may be professionally dry cleaned or hand-washed in cold water with pH-neutral soap.
  • Do not machine dry on high heat; air dry flat in the shade to prevent warping.
First-Aid Guide

Emergency Stain Removal Protocols

Act swiftly: 90% of stains can be completely erased if blotted within the first 3 minutes.

Stain Type Immediate First Action Artisan Cleaning Solution
Red Wine / Fruit Juice Blot repeatedly with dry white towel. Do not rub. Mix 1 part white vinegar + 2 parts lukewarm water with a drop of mild dish soap. Dab gently from outside edge inward.
Coffee / Tea Blot excess liquid immediately. Dab with mild wool detergent mixed with cold water. Blot dry, then place a weighted towel over the spot for 2 hours.
Oils / Grease / Butter Scrape solids with a dull butter knife. Dust generously with cornstarch or talcum powder. Allow 4 hours to absorb grease, then vacuum suction-only.
Pet Accidents Blot thoroughly with layered paper towels. Apply a solution of 1/4 cup white vinegar + 2 cups cold water to neutralize odor and acid. Rinse with cold water dab.
Candle Wax Allow wax to harden fully (use ice cube). Gently shatter and pick off chunks. Place brown paper bag over remaining residue and lightly press with warm (not hot) iron.
Critical Precautions

What Never to Do to a Handwoven Rug

No Steam Cleaners

High-temperature pressurized steam forces moisture deep into the inner wool foundation warps, causing dry rot and fiber mold.

No Supermarket Chemical Sprays

Bleach, ammonia, and foam stain removers chemically strip natural vegetable dyes and leave permanent bleached halos.

Never Vacuum The Fringe

The fringe cords are the structural warp foundation of the rug. Vacuum heads catch and unravel end knots instantly.

Need Museum-Grade Rug Cleaning?

We recommend submerged artisan wash every 3 to 5 years. Our conservators offer white-glove pickup, pH-neutral basin washing, and flat-drying.
