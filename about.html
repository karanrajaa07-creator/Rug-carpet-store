Our Heritage

Honoring The Loom, Empowering The Artisan

Founded on the belief that a truly exceptional rug is not manufactured — it is born from patience, natural mountain fibers, and generational hand-knotting pedigree.

Hand-Knotted with Honor Fair-trade certified weaver collectives
Since 2008

Where Architectural Vision Meets Antique Loom Mastery

For nearly two decades, Rug & Carpet Store has served as the bridge between historic weaver villages across Persia, the Atlas Mountains, Anatolia, and Varanasi, and the world’s most refined architectural residences.

Our Mission

To ensure ancient hand-weaving arts thrive in modern homes by producing non-toxic, bespoke heirloom textiles of unyielding quality.

Our Vision

To lead the luxury floorcovering world in zero-carbon handlooms, 100% biodegradable natural dyes, and lifelong restoration care.

Milestones

The Evolution of Our Atelier

2008
The Genesis

Founded as a boutique curator of rare semi-antique Persian and Caucasian runner rugs in Soho, New York.

2016
Direct Guild Alliances

Established direct ethical partnerships with 50+ weaver families across Morocco, Turkey, and Kashmir, bypassing middleman markups.

2026
The Digital Atelier

Launched our proprietary custom sizing studio and white-glove global delivery network serving penthouses and villas across 40 countries.

Pillars of Integrity

Our Guiding Values

Ethical Fair Compensation

Our weavers receive living wages, healthcare endowments, and safe daylight loom workshops.

Vegetable Mineral Dyes

Walnut shells, madder root, indigo, and pomegranate rinds create timeless abrash shades that never emit VOCs.

Century Durability

Double-weft hand-knotted architecture designed to outlive machine-made rugs by several generations.

100% Biodegradable

Crafted from earth-given wool, cotton, and silk fibers that return organically to the soil without synthetic microplastics.
